ABOUT HACKERBOOST

HackerBoost is a leading tech education and software development organization based in Tamale, Ghana. Their mission is to empower young African developers with practical digital skills, moving the continent from tech consumption to value creation.

Core services

Tech Training & Bootcamps: They offer guided learning in fields such as Blockchain Development, Artificial Intelligence, React, and foundational computer literacy.

Software Development & Consultancy: The organization builds real-world technology solutions, mobile/web platforms, and internal systems for businesses and institutions

Community & Mentorship: HackerBoost fosters a vibrant community of developers through hackathons, events, and career mentorship.

Mission and Vision

mission

To accelerate tech careers through certified, industry-aligned training programs, practical execution, and collaborative community building. HackerBoost aims to empower one million professionals with digital skills by 2030 and shift Africa from a tech-consuming continent to a tech-value-creating one.

Vision

To equip youth with advanced digital and technological skills (including Web3, Blockchain, AI, and Software Development) that foster sustainable economic development, financial freedom, and real-world problem solving.
